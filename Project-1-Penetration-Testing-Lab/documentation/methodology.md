Penetration Testing Methodology
 1. Reconnaissance
The assessment began with network discovery and identification of the target system.
 2. Enumeration
Nmap was used to identify open ports, services, service versions, and other information exposed by the target.
Additional enumeration tools were used where appropriate.

3. Vulnerability Identification
The identified services and versions were reviewed to determine potential vulnerabilities.

4. Vulnerability Validation
Selected vulnerabilities were validated within the isolated Metasploitable 2 laboratory environment.

5. Evidence Collection
Screenshots, scan results, command output, and other relevant evidence were collected throughout the assessment.

6. Reporting
The findings were documented with their associated evidence, impact, and recommended remediation
